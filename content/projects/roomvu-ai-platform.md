## Full-Stack & AI Software Engineering at Roomvu

Developed across Roomvu's three-service platform: a Laravel 12 (PHP 8.4) core API with MariaDB and Redis, a Next.js/TypeScript agent dashboard, and a FastAPI, Celery and Prefect video-generation microservice.

### AI Property Tour Videos
Built immersive property-tour videos with vertical/horizontal aspect ratios, pluggable overlay templates, cinematic camera styles, and lip-synced talking-head narration using generative video models (Veo, Vidu, OmniHuman). Optimized rendering with ffmpeg post-processing.

### Voice Cloning Engine
Led a second voice-cloning engine (Fish Audio) alongside ElevenLabs end to end: async cloning, side-by-side engine comparison UI, staged rollout from test users to 10%, 30%, and all agents, plus backfill tooling.

### Automated Content Pipeline
Extended the automated local-news and explainer video pipeline: Remotion templates, per-scene narration, weighted multi-dimension footage selection with prompt retries, and an admin panel showing per-run and per-video outcomes.

### AI Image & Video Processing
Implemented background removal and replacement (BiRefNet, RMBG), validation of agent body-shot photos, and an avatar-capture flow that turns selfie recordings into voice clones and AI avatars.
